Menu Highlights

Our menu is built on home-style cooking,. A few guest favorites:

  • Breakfast (served all day): the Bear Special, the Plateful, three-egg omelets including our Mediterranean omelet, eggs Benedict, skillets, breakfast burritos, homemade corned beef hash, biscuits and gravy, and stuffed French toast. Pancakes, French toast and waffles are served until 2:00 PM daily.
  • Lunch: 1/3-pound burgers (including the Bonduel Burger and a brat burger) on regular, pretzel or gluten-free buns, gourmet chicken sandwiches, melts, wraps, fresh salads and homemade soup and chili by the cup, bowl or to-go quart.
  • Wisconsin appetizers: white and garlic cheese curds, deep-fried pickles, onion rings, breaded mushrooms and a pick-any-five sampler platter.
  • Dinner: ribeye and T-bone steaks, grilled pork chops, homemade meatloaf, country fried steak, stuffed chicken breast dinners, grilled salmon and jumbo shrimp, each with soup or salad, potato or wild rice, the vegetable of the day and a dinner roll.
  • Desserts: homemade pie, torte and cake by the slice (add ice cream à la mode), whole pies to take home, hand-scooped ice cream, shakes, malts and sundaes.

We also offer a kids’ and senior menu (under 12 and over 60) for breakfast, lunch and dinner, and gluten-friendly options marked GF on the menu — most breakfast items can be made gluten friendly on request.

Fish Fry Friday

Friday is fish fry night in Wisconsin, and at Hungry Bear it’s a tradition. We stay open until 8:00 PM every Friday so families from Bonduel, Shawano and the surrounding area can sit down to a classic Friday fish fry.

  • Crispy deep-fried haddock dinners
  • Breaded haddock fish sandwich on a hoagie bun
  • Smelt basket, popcorn shrimp and beer-battered shrimp baskets with fries and coleslaw
  • Grilled salmon — lemon pepper, Cajun or plain — for a lighter option
  • Homemade chowder, served Fridays only

Call (715) 758-2727 to place a to-go order.

Catering in Bonduel & Shawano County

Hungry Bear caters weddings, graduation open houses, birthday parties, baby and bridal showers, holiday parties and corporate meetings throughout Bonduel, Shawano, Gresham, Clintonville, Gillett and Pulaski. Our kitchen has handled events of around 100 guests, and every menu is planned with you — from home-style entrées and sides to homemade pies and desserts for the whole crowd.

To get a quote, call (715) 758-2727 or email sarahhungrybear2@gmail.com with your date, guest count and the kind of food you have in mind. Popular dates such as May–June graduation weekends and December holiday parties fill up early.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Hungry Bear's hours?

We are open Tuesday through Thursday 6:30 AM to 7:00 PM, Friday 6:30 AM to 8:00 PM, Saturday 6:30 AM to 4:00 PM and Sunday 6:30 AM to 2:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays. Holiday hours may vary, so please call (715) 758-2727.

Where is Hungry Bear Cafe & Catering located?

We are at 120 W. Green Bay St., Bonduel, WI 54107, across the street from Village Hall and three blocks south of the high school.

Do you serve breakfast all day?

Yes. Breakfast is served whenever we are open. Pancakes, French toast and waffles are available until 2:00 PM daily.

When is Fish Fry Friday?

Every Friday. We stay open until 8:00 PM and serve deep-fried haddock, smelt and shrimp baskets, grilled salmon and our homemade chowder, which is served on Fridays only.

Do you offer catering?

Yes. We cater weddings, graduation parties, showers, birthdays, holiday parties and corporate meetings in Bonduel, Shawano and nearby towns, including events of around 100 guests. Call (715) 758-2727 or email sarahhungrybear2@gmail.com for a quote.

Can I order a whole homemade pie?

Yes. Whole homemade pies are available to order. Call ahead to ask about the flavors we are baking that week, especially before Thanksgiving and Christmas.

Do you have gluten-friendly or kids' options?

Gluten-friendly items are marked GF on our menu, burgers can be served on a gluten-free bun and most breakfast items can be made gluten friendly on request. We also have a kids' and senior menu for guests under 12 and over 60.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, checks, American Express, Discover, MasterCard and Visa.
