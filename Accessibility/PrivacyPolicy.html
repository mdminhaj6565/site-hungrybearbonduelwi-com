AMP collects information relating to you and your use of the Site and Services in order to provide services and features that are responsive to your needs. We collect personal information in the following ways:

Via our authorized Merchant-based URLs, we collect information from you when you register an account with us, submit reviews, testimonials, content, or information to the Site, fill out a form, complete a survey, request information, send us an email or other communication, speak with us on the telephone, or otherwise interact with us or the Site or Services.
