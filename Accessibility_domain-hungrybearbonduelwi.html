About Us

Hungry Bear - Cafe & Catering in Bonduel, WI, is your ultimate destination for a delightful dining experience. Serving the Bonduel community with pride, we are known for our delicious homemade pies and freshly baked treats, perfectly paired with our hearty breakfast, lunch, and dinner options. Enjoy your meal on our charming outdoor patio or choose our outstanding catering services for your special events, where every dish is crafted to impress. Visit Hungry Bear - Cafe & Catering for tasty bites and memorable gatherings.

What We Offer

Our Specialties include:

  • We provide catering for all your events, including weddings, birthday and graduation parties, showers, and corporate meetings. Trust us for delicious dishes and exceptional service to make your event memorable.

  • Experience delightful dining all day at Hungry Bear. Start with hearty breakfast classics, savor gourmet lunches with crisp salads and sandwiches, and indulge in comforting dinner entrées. Our talented chefs ensure each meal is a culinary treat, from morning to night.

Fall at Hungry Bear: Patio Days, Fish Fry Fridays and Holiday Pies

Fall has arrived in Bonduel. Grab a patio table while the weather holds for breakfast or lunch, then warm up inside with a bowl of our homemade soup or chili. On Fridays we stay open until 8:00 PM for Fish Fry Friday, with our homemade chowder served Fridays only.
