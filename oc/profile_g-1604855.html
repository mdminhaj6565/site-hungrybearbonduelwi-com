"Come Hungry - Leave Happy!" Hungry Bear - Cafe & Catering is a family restaurant at 120 W. Green Bay St. in downtown Bonduel, WI, serving home-style breakfast, lunch and dinner since 2015. Guests come for three-egg omelets, skillets and eggs Benedict, 1/3-pound burgers, homemade soup and chili, and dinner favorites like ribeye steak, pork chops and homemade meatloaf. Every Friday we stay open until 8:00 PM for Fish Fry Friday, and our homemade pies are available by the slice or as whole pies to take home. We also cater weddings, graduation parties, showers and corporate events for Bonduel, Shawano, Gresham, Clintonville, Gillett and Pulaski. Call (715) 758-2727 for hours, catering quotes or pie orders.
